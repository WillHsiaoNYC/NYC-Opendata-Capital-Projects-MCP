Funding can precede a schedule

Budget lines can exist before Design, or for work that does not require schedule reporting. A line without a linked PID can be normal.

A financial measure

The spending ratio is not a construction completion percentage. These budgets span projects and years; this is not one year’s City capital budget.

START WITH THE QUESTION.
THEN CHOOSE THE LENS.

“When will it be delivered?”

Find its PID, then inspect schedule dates, lifecycle and reporting-period changes.

“How much funding is reported?”

Find the budget-holder + FMS ID pair. Inspect every linked schedule and budget.

“What is this agency’s portfolio?”

Choose owner/sponsor or builder/manager before counting or comparing.
